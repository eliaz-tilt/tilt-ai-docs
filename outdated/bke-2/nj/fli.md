New Jersey Family Leave Insurance (NJ FLI)

NJ FLI intermittent leave is capped at 56 individual calendar days (8 weeks)
within the applicable 12-month benefit period.

How the bank is reduced depends on the pattern of leave taken:

• If an employee takes intermittent leave for only certain days within a week
(for example, Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday), only those calendar days are
deducted from the 56-day bank.

• If an employee is out for their entire scheduled workweek (for example, Monday
through Friday for a typical five-day workweek), NJ counts the associated
weekend days as part of the leave period, resulting in a 7-day deduction from
the 56-day bank.

• If an employee works fewer days per week (for example, a three-day workweek)
and takes all of their scheduled workdays off in a week, that still counts as a
full week of leave, or 7 calendar days, leaving 49 days remaining.

If the employee is eligible, I believe LSM’s use an employee’s pay history and
an internal calculator to estimate benefit amount. This process belongs to the
CS team, and they could help work through the remaining steps, which likely
includes checking whether the employee has already used any/all of these
benefits within their benefit year. (They may not have access to all of this
info if the employee is a recent hire that previously worked at another
company.) The state or private insurance company will ultimately decide an
employee’s eligibility and benefit amounts after receiving and reviewing the
employee’s application.

Potential length of FLI benefit: for continuous leave, up to 12 weeks in a 12
month period; for intermittent leave, up to 56 individual days (8 weeks) in a 12
month period. Intermittent leave must be used in increments of at least one
day - NJ FLI doesn’t apply on any partially worked days. There is no waiting
period for FLI.
